# Product Management Resources

> **TL;DR** — Curated books, people, newsletters, communities, tools and standards for product management and ownership. If you read only five: *Inspired* (Cagan), *Continuous Discovery Habits* (Torres), *Escaping the Build Trap* (Perri), *User Story Mapping* (Patton), *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments* (Kohavi et al.). Links are to canonical homepages or Wikipedia.

---
