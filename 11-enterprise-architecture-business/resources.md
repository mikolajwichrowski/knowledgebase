# Resources: Books, Standards, Sites, Tools

> **TL;DR** The short list: *Enterprise Architecture as Strategy* (Ross, Weill, Robertson) for why EA matters; the TOGAF and ArchiMate specs for method and notation; *BPMN Method and Style* (Silver) for process modeling; *Fundamentals of BPM* for process management; *Learning to See* for value streams; *Good Strategy/Bad Strategy* and *Playing to Win* for strategy; Wardley's free book for maps; *The Trusted Advisor* and *Flawless Consulting* for consulting. Standards bodies: The Open Group, OMG, IIBA, PeopleCert/AXELOS, ISACA, ISO, EU Commission.

---
