# Books, Standards and Resources

> **TL;DR** The short list: PMBOK Guide (7th/8th) and PRINCE2 for vocabulary and governance; Flyvbjerg's *How Big Things Get Done* for why big projects fail; *The Mythical Man-Month* for software dynamics; *Critical Chain* for scheduling behaviour; McConnell's *Software Estimation* and Magennis/Vacanti for forecasting; *Waltzing with Bears* for risk; and the vendor implementation guides (SAP Activate, Dynamics 365 Success by Design) for ERP.

---
