## 6. Suggested learning paths

### New tech lead (first 90 days)

1. *The Manager's Path* — tech lead chapters.
2. [Feedback, 1:1s & conflict](feedback-1on1s-conflict.md) — start 1:1s using the templates.
3. [Motivation & leadership](motivation-and-leadership.md) — run Moving Motivators and Delegation Poker with the team.
4. [Workshop facilitation](workshop-facilitation.md) — facilitate the next retro using 1-2-4-All.
5. [Meetings & async](meetings-async-and-effectiveness.md) — audit your calendar.

### Architect / Staff+ moving into org influence

1. *Staff Engineer*, *The Software Architect Elevator*.
2. [Team design](team-design.md) — map teams and interactions.
3. [Communication & stakeholders](communication-and-stakeholders.md) — Minto, BLUF, tech debt business case.
4. [Decision making](decision-making.md) — introduce DACI + ADRs.
5. [Change management](change-management.md) — plan adoption with Rogers + ADKAR.

### Consultant / coordinator

1. *Flawless Consulting*, *The Trusted Advisor*.
2. [Coaching & consulting](coaching-and-consulting.md) — contracting, issue trees.
3. [Facilitation methods catalog](facilitation-methods-catalog.md) — Liberating Structures, Event Storming.
4. [Communication & stakeholders](communication-and-stakeholders.md) — culture map, negotiation.
