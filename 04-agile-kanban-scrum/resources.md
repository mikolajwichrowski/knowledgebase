# Resources: Agile, Kanban, Scrum, Lean and Flow

> **TL;DR** — Curated books, official guides, sites and tools, grouped by topic, with a suggested reading path. Official sources first (agilemanifesto.org, scrumguides.org, kanbanguides.org, kanban.university), then the canonical books. For Kanban/flow depth: Anderson (*Kanban*), Vacanti (*Actionable Agile Metrics*, *When Will It Be Done?*), Reinertsen (*Principles of Product Development Flow*), Goldratt (*The Goal*). For Scrum: the Scrum Guide itself, then practitioner books. For engineering: Beck (*XP Explained*), Humble & Farley (*Continuous Delivery*), Forsgren et al. (*Accelerate*).

## 6. Suggested reading paths

**Path A — Kanban & flow (team lead / delivery lead)**
1. The Kanban Guide (1 hour)
2. *Essential Kanban Condensed*
3. *Making Work Visible*
4. *Actionable Agile Metrics for Predictability*
5. *When Will It Be Done?*
6. *Kanban* (Anderson) — for change management and cadences
7. *The Principles of Product Development Flow* — economic depth
8. *Rethinking Agile* — scaling flow

**Path B — Scrum done well**
1. Scrum Guide 2020
2. *Scrum — A Pocket Guide*
3. *User Story Mapping*
4. *Agile Retrospectives*
5. *The Zombie Scrum Survival Guide*

**Path C — Lean & systems**
1. *This Is Lean*
2. *The Goal* → *The Phoenix Project*
3. *Lean Software Development*
4. *Value Stream Mapping*
5. *Project to Product*

**Path D — Engineering for agility**
1. *Extreme Programming Explained*
2. *Continuous Delivery*
3. *Accelerate*
4. *Team Topologies*
