# Resources

## Books by theme

### Software engineering laws and craft
- **Fred Brooks, *The Mythical Man-Month* (1975/1995)** — Brooks's law, second-system effect, "no silver bullet". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mythical_Man-Month
- Hunt & Thomas, *The Pragmatic Programmer* (1999/2019) — DRY, broken windows, rubber ducking. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pragmatic_Programmer
- John Gall, *Systemantics / The Systems Bible* (1975/2002). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systemantics
- Gerald Weinberg, *The Psychology of Computer Programming* (1971), *The Secrets of Consulting* (1985). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerald_Weinberg
- Winters, Manshreck & Wright, *Software Engineering at Google* (2020) — Hyrum's law, scale. https://www.hyrumslaw.com/
- Martin Fowler, *Refactoring* (1999/2018) — rule of three. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_refactoring
- Mike Gancarz, *The UNIX Philosophy* (1994). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unix_philosophy

### Organisations, teams and management
- **DeMarco & Lister, *Peopleware* (1987)**. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peopleware:_Productive_Projects_and_Teams
- Skelton & Pais, *Team Topologies* (2019). https://teamtopologies.com/
- Forsgren, Humble & Kim, *Accelerate* (2018). https://dora.dev/
- C. Northcote Parkinson, *Parkinson's Law* (1957). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkinson%27s_law
- Laurence J. Peter, *The Peter Principle* (1969). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_principle
- Amy Edmondson, *The Fearless Organization* (2018). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_safety
- Daniel Pink, *Drive* (2009). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drive:_The_Surprising_Truth_About_What_Motivates_Us
- Peter Drucker, *The Effective Executive* (1967). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Drucker

### Cognitive biases and judgement
- **Daniel Kahneman, *Thinking, Fast and Slow* (2011)**. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking,_Fast_and_Slow
- Kahneman, Sibony & Sunstein, *Noise* (2021). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noise:_A_Flaw_in_Human_Judgment
- Tetlock & Gardner, *Superforecasting* (2015). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superforecasting:_The_Art_and_Science_of_Prediction
- Annie Duke, *Thinking in Bets* (2018).
- Gary Klein, *Sources of Power* (1998) — naturalistic decision making; pre-mortem. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_A._Klein
- Bent Flyvbjerg & Dan Gardner, *How Big Things Get Done* (2023) — reference class forecasting. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bent_Flyvbjerg
- Douglas Hubbard, *How to Measure Anything* (2007/2014).

### Mental models and decision-making

- Robert Axelrod, *The Evolution of Cooperation* (1984). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Evolution_of_Cooperation
